about

Everyday requests, handled on hardware you control.

Mahasamuha is a personal assistant for Android in early development. It handles everyday requests by voice or text, and it works only with a backend server that you run.

texts

Send a text to someone in your contacts by saying who it’s for and what it says.

weather

Ask about the weather where you are, using your approximate location.

speakers

Find speakers on your home network and cast to them.

Longer-term memory, where the assistant carries useful context from one conversation into the next, is in development.
